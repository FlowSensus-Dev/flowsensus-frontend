## ⚠️ Common Demo Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **DON'T** skip medical clearance and go straight to CV → CV will be locked  
❌ **DON'T** try to access Document OCR before employer acceptance → Will be locked  
❌ **DON'T** forget to click "Save" after selecting "Fit to Work" → Medical won't be recorded  
❌ **DON'T** skip the Smart Profiling "Run Matching" button → Job matches won't appear  
❌ **DON'T** rush through the Master Key moment → This is the climax of the demo  

✅ **DO** read the info banners aloud  
✅ **DO** point out toast notifications  
✅ **DO** emphasize when modules unlock  
✅ **DO** show the applicant portal at the end

## 🆘 Emergency Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
